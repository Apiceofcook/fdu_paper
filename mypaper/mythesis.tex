\begin{abstract}
% 在此处撰写中文摘要
% 摘要应简明扼要地概括论文的主要内容、研究方法、主要结论等
% 建议字数：300-500字

多模态大语言模型在通用视觉理解任务上快速发展，但训练数据普遍存在噪声大、对齐弱与推理密度不足等问题，使得“数据质量”逐渐成为能力提升瓶颈。本文围绕“以可控、可迭代、可验证的方式生产高质量训练信号”展开研究，沿“\textbf{数据能力光谱}”的三个代表性位置形成“两端实证 $\rightarrow$ 共通抽象 $\rightarrow$ 综合中段独立验证”的研究路径：（1）\textbf{感知端——面向移动端 GUI 的高精度可执行监督}。提出以多源数据融合为核心的 GUI 数据工程 Pipeline，构建包含紧致 bbox、OCR 对齐、控件关系图谱与状态向量等结构化监督，并结合 AnyRes 高分辨率感知、文本化坐标表示与双阶段混合微调（DMT）提升 Referring/Grounding 等关键能力；在 Top 200 主流应用真实分布评测中，融合开源清洗与闭源精标数据将 Grounding F1 由 0.324 提升至 0.846、Referring F1 由 0.523 提升至 0.905。（2）\textbf{推理端——面向世界知识的可验证对齐与过程推理}。建立 7 大类 40 子类的知识类别框架与端到端数据 Pipeline，通过“词条$\rightarrow$图片$\rightarrow$caption$\rightarrow$相关性分数过滤$\rightarrow$RecQA/KnowQA/FinalQA”将分散知识转化为可学习、可校验的多模态监督；并在 FinalQA 中引入 Think/CoT 过程监督，缓解“短路映射”并强化“先识别再推理”能力，在 Chinese SimpleVQA 与 SimpleVQA 上取得有竞争力的结果。（3）\textbf{共通抽象——Auto-Evol 多模态数据合成框架}。从两端的工程实践中归纳共通骨架，提出闭环数据合成系统：通过数据源组织与结构化初始化、能力路由与原子问题生成（感知/理解/推理三链路）、代理式增强、多维过滤与评测式校验、失败样本回流重写/重生成，稳定产出高信息密度、高对齐度与高推理密度的训练信号。（4）\textbf{综合中段——GUI CUA 端到端独立验证}。GUI 计算机使用智能体（Computer Use Agent, CUA）需要“感知 + 推理 + 状态机”三者协同，处于光谱的综合中段，与第 3 章单步定位的 GUI 任务存在本质区别。本文将 Auto-Evol 实例化到 OSWorld LibreOffice 三个 domain（Calc/Impress/Writer），通过“900 合成任务 $\rightarrow$ 723 成功轨迹 $\rightarrow$ 3 万条单步训练数据 $\rightarrow$ Qwen3VL-Think SFT”的端到端实验，将模型 overall accuracy 由 40.8\% 提升至 61.4\%（+20.6 pt 绝对增益），证明 Auto-Evol 确实可跨越“感知端 $\rightarrow$ 综合中段”完成迁移而非仅做归纳总结。两端实证与综合中段独立实验共同支撑了该框架的\textbf{双重定位}：既是对两端工作的方法论闭合，亦在新场景上提供独立的可验证端到端贡献，为多模态数据合成在更多任务域的复用与扩展提供了通用底座。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
% Write your English abstract here
% The abstract should briefly summarize the main content, research methods, 
% and conclusions of the thesis
% Recommended length: 200-400 words

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have advanced rapidly in general vision understanding, yet their progress is increasingly bottlenecked by training data quality, including noise, weak vision--language alignment, and insufficient reasoning density. This thesis investigates how to produce high-quality multimodal training signals in a controllable, iterative, and verifiable manner, organized around a \textbf{data capability spectrum} that motivates a four-step research path: \emph{two-end empirical study $\rightarrow$ common abstraction $\rightarrow$ mid-band independent validation}.

\textbf{(1) Perception end --- high-precision executable supervision for mobile GUIs.} We build a multi-source data engineering Pipeline with structured, executable supervision (tight bounding boxes, OCR alignment, element relation graphs, and interaction states), combined with AnyRes high-resolution perception, text-formatted coordinate generation, and a dual-stage mixed tuning (DMT) strategy. On a top-200 popular-application evaluation, this yields large gains (Grounding F1: 0.324 $\rightarrow$ 0.846; Referring F1: 0.523 $\rightarrow$ 0.905).

\textbf{(2) Reasoning end --- verifiable alignment and process supervision for world knowledge.} We construct a taxonomy (7 super-categories, 40 sub-categories) and an end-to-end Pipeline ``entry $\rightarrow$ images $\rightarrow$ captions $\rightarrow$ relevance scoring $\rightarrow$ RecQA/KnowQA/FinalQA'' that turns dispersed knowledge into learnable and checkable multimodal supervision. Think/CoT-style process supervision in FinalQA mitigates shortcut learning and strengthens transferable ``recognize-then-reason'' capability, achieving competitive results on Chinese SimpleVQA and SimpleVQA.

\textbf{(3) Common abstraction --- the Auto-Evol framework.} Generalizing recurring patterns from both ends, we propose Auto-Evol, a closed-loop multimodal data synthesis framework integrating structured initialization, task routing with atomic question construction (perception/understanding/reasoning chains), agent-based augmentation, multi-dimensional filtering and evaluation-driven verification, and feedback-driven rewriting, to stably produce training signals of high information density, alignment, and reasoning density.

\textbf{(4) Synthetic mid-band --- end-to-end independent validation via GUI CUA.} GUI Computer Use Agent (CUA) tasks require coordinated perception, reasoning, and state-machine tracking and thus reside in the \emph{synthetic mid-band} of the data capability spectrum, fundamentally different from the single-step Mobile GUI tasks in Chapter 3. We instantiate Auto-Evol over three OSWorld LibreOffice domains (Calc/Impress/Writer) and conduct an end-to-end ``900 synthetic tasks $\rightarrow$ 723 successful trajectories $\rightarrow$ 30k single-step training samples $\rightarrow$ Qwen3VL-Think SFT'' experiment, raising overall accuracy from 40.8\% to 61.4\% (+20.6 pt absolute gain). This demonstrates that Auto-Evol genuinely \emph{transfers} from the perception end to the synthetic mid-band rather than merely summarizing earlier work.

Together, the two-end empirical study and the mid-band independent experiment establish the \textbf{dual positioning} of Auto-Evol: a methodological closure of the two task lines and an independently verifiable end-to-end contribution in a new agentic scenario. The proposed framework and data engineering methodology effectively improve localization, understanding, and knowledge reasoning in realistic settings, and offer a reusable substrate for broader multimodal task domains across the data capability spectrum.

\end{abstract*}
