\begin{abstract}
% 在此处撰写中文摘要
% 摘要应简明扼要地概括论文的主要内容、研究方法、主要结论等
% 建议字数：300-500字

多模态大语言模型近年来快速发展，但其能力提升正越来越受限于训练数据的质量、对齐度与推理密度。互联网抓取的图文对噪声较大、对齐较弱；人工标注成本高、长尾覆盖有限；模型在真实任务中暴露出小目标定位不稳、视觉证据与语言描述绑定松散、推理任务易出现短路映射等结构性误差。“数据规模优先”的合成范式已遇瓶颈，亟需面向质量与能力维度的系统化数据生产方法。

本文研究如何以可控、可迭代、可验证的方式生产高质量多模态训练数据，并沿数据能力光谱的感知端、推理端与综合中段三个代表性位置展开实证。在感知端面向图形用户界面（GUI）任务，构建以多源融合为核心的数据合成流水线，结合紧致边界框、光学字符识别（OCR）对齐、控件关系图谱与状态向量等结构化监督，并配合动态分辨率感知（AnyRes）与双阶段混合微调策略，在覆盖前 200 主流应用真实分布的内部评测集上将视觉定位 F1 分数由 0.324 提升至 0.846、指代识别 F1 分数由 0.523 提升至 0.905。在推理端面向世界知识任务，建立 7 大类 40 子类知识类别框架与端到端数据流水线，通过“词条$\rightarrow$图片$\rightarrow$图像描述$\rightarrow$相关性过滤$\rightarrow$识别问答（RecQA）/\allowbreak 知识问答（KnowQA）/\allowbreak 综合问答（FinalQA）”链路将分散知识转化为可学习、可校验的多模态监督，并在综合问答中引入思维链（Chain-of-Thought, CoT）过程监督以缓解短路映射，在 Chinese SimpleVQA 与 SimpleVQA 上取得有竞争力的结果。

在两端工程实践基础上，本文进一步抽象提出 Auto-Evol 多模态数据合成框架，将以往单步生成升级为包含数据源接入、结构化初始化、质量过滤、原子问题生成、数据增强、多维校验与失败回流共七个模块的闭环系统，以“感知/理解/推理”三能力为统一坐标系。为验证框架的可迁移性，本文将其实例化到光谱综合中段的图形用户界面计算机使用智能体（GUI CUA）场景，依托 OSWorld 评测体系在 LibreOffice 三个应用领域上构建可验证任务合成系统；通过 900 合成任务、723 成功轨迹与 3 万条单步训练数据，将 Qwen3-VL-Think 模型在 OSWorld LibreOffice 三个领域上的总体准确率由 40.8\% 提升至 61.4\%，绝对增益 20.6 个百分点。所提方法与框架为多模态数据合成在更多任务域的复用与扩展提供了可参考的工程范式。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
% Write your English abstract here
% The abstract should briefly summarize the main content, research methods,
% and conclusions of the thesis
% Recommended length: 200-400 words

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have advanced rapidly in vision--language understanding, but their capability ceiling is increasingly constrained by the quality, alignment, and reasoning density of training data rather than data scale. Web-crawled image--text pairs are noisy and weakly aligned; manual annotation is expensive and narrow in long-tail coverage; models exhibit structural errors such as unstable small-object localization, loose vision--language grounding, and shortcut learning on reasoning tasks. Under this bottleneck, the traditional ``scale-first'' synthesis paradigm is no longer sufficient.

This thesis investigates how to produce high-quality multimodal training data in a controllable, iterative, and verifiable manner, organized along three representative positions on the data capability spectrum: the perception end, the reasoning end, and the synthetic mid-band. At the perception end, targeting mobile GUI tasks, we build a multi-source data synthesis pipeline with structured supervision (tight bounding boxes, OCR alignment, element relation graphs, interaction states), combined with AnyRes high-resolution perception and dual-stage mixed tuning. On an internal evaluation set covering the real-world distribution of top-200 popular applications, this raises Grounding F1 from 0.324 to 0.846 and Referring F1 from 0.523 to 0.905. At the reasoning end, targeting world-knowledge tasks, we construct a taxonomy of 7 super-categories and 40 sub-categories with an end-to-end pipeline (entry $\rightarrow$\allowbreak\ images $\rightarrow$\allowbreak\ captions $\rightarrow$\allowbreak\ relevance filtering $\rightarrow$\allowbreak\ RecQA/\allowbreak KnowQA/\allowbreak FinalQA), and introduce Think/CoT process supervision in FinalQA to mitigate shortcut learning, achieving competitive results on Chinese SimpleVQA and SimpleVQA.

Generalizing recurring patterns from both ends, we propose Auto-Evol, a closed-loop multimodal data synthesis framework with seven modules---data sourcing, structured initialization, quality filtering, atomic-question generation, data augmentation, multi-dimensional validation, and feedback rewriting---organized around the perception/\allowbreak understanding/\allowbreak reasoning capability axes. To validate transferability, we instantiate Auto-Evol on the synthetic mid-band---GUI Computer Use Agent (CUA) tasks---and build a verifiable-task synthesis pipeline on three OSWorld LibreOffice domains under the OSWorld benchmark. With 900 synthetic tasks, 723 successful trajectories, and 30k single-step training samples, Qwen3-VL-Think achieves an overall accuracy improvement from 40.8\% to 61.4\% (+20.6 points). The proposed methodology and framework provide a reusable engineering paradigm for multimodal data synthesis across broader task domains.

\end{abstract*}
